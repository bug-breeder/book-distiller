 About the Authors 
 Russell A. Barkley, PhD, ABPP, ABCN, is internationally known for his career-long research into ADHD and his efforts to educate professionals and the public. He is Clinical Professor of Psychiatry at the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine. The recipient of awards from the American Academy of Pediatrics and the American Psychological Association, among other honors, Dr. Barkley has published and lectured widely on ADHD and related disorders. His website is www.russellbarkley.org . 
 Christine M. Benton is a Chicago-based writer and editor.
